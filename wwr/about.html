The Right Rafting Corp.

We exist to guide adventurers of all skill levels through nature’s most breathtaking rivers while promoting environmental stewardship and river conservation.

History

Founded in 2012 by passionate local river guides, The Right Rafting Corp. began with just two rafts and a dream. Today, we continue guiding thousands down wild, breathtaking rapids safely every year.

Over the past decade, our small local outfit has expanded into a premier river adventure company. Throughout our growth, we remain deeply committed to river conservation and supporting our surrounding mountain communities.
